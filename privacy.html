Privacy policy

Datenschutzerklärung. Information under Art. 13 GDPR on how personal data is processed when you visit this website.

In short

This website sets no cookies, uses no analytics or tracking and has no contact form. Fonts and scripts are served from this website itself, so your browser does not contact any other provider. The only personal data processed is the technical data your browser sends to the hosting provider when it loads a page.

2. Hosting and server logs

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. St., San Francisco, CA 94107, USA.

To deliver a page, your browser has to send technical data to GitHub's servers. This includes your IP address, the date and time of the request, the page requested, the referring page and information about your browser and operating system. According to GitHub, the IP address of every visitor to a GitHub Pages site is logged and stored for security purposes. I have no access to these logs and do not receive any visitor statistics.

  • Purpose: delivering the website and keeping the service secure and stable.
  • Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR. My legitimate interest is to present this website reliably and securely.
  • Transfer to the USA: GitHub participates in the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, for which the European Commission has adopted an adequacy decision (Art. 45 GDPR). GitHub also uses the EU standard contractual clauses (Art. 46 GDPR).
  • Encryption: connections to this website are encrypted with HTTPS (TLS), which protects the transferred data from being read by third parties.
  • Storage period: set by GitHub. Details are in the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

3. No cookies, no tracking, no external content

This website does not use cookies, local storage, analytics, social media plugins or advertising. Fonts (Space Grotesk, IBM Plex) and the three.js library used for the 3D illustration are stored on this website and loaded from here, not from Google or another content delivery network.

4. Links to other websites

This website links to other websites, for example LinkedIn, GitHub, arXiv, Zenodo, Google Colab and the CrashDefNet documentation. No data is sent to these providers until you click a link. Once you follow a link, the privacy policy of that website applies.

5. Contact by email

If you write to me by email, I process your email address and the content of your message to answer you. The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR (my legitimate interest in answering enquiries) or, if your enquiry concerns a contract or job offer, Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR. I delete the messages once they are no longer needed, unless statutory retention periods apply.

6. Your rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access (Art. 15 GDPR)
  • Rectification (Art. 16 GDPR)
  • Erasure (Art. 17 GDPR)
  • Restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR)
  • Data portability (Art. 20 GDPR)
  • Objection (Art. 21 GDPR), see section 7

To exercise these rights, an email to the address above is enough. Note that I can only act on data I hold myself; the server logs are held by GitHub.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR), in particular in the EU member state of your habitual residence, your place of work or the place of the alleged infringement. The authority responsible for private individuals in Bavaria is the Bayerisches Landesamt für Datenschutzaufsicht (BayLDA), Promenade 18, 91522 Ansbach, Germany.

7. Your right to object (Art. 21 GDPR)

Where I process data on the basis of Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR (legitimate interest), you have the right to object to this processing at any time on grounds relating to your particular situation. I will then no longer process the data concerned, unless I can demonstrate compelling legitimate grounds for the processing which override your interests, rights and freedoms, or the processing serves the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims. An email to the address above is enough to object.

8. Other information

You are not required to provide any personal data. Without the technical data described in section 2, however, the website cannot be delivered. There is no automated decision-making or profiling.
