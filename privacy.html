Privacy policy

Datenschutzerklärung. Information under Art. 13 GDPR on how personal data is processed when you visit this skill profile or the CrashDefNet documentation.

In short

This privacy policy covers two websites: this skill profile (stofe94.github.io/skill-profile) and the CrashDefNet documentation (stofe94.github.io/crash-def-net). Neither sets cookies, uses analytics or tracking, or has a contact form. Fonts and scripts are served from the websites themselves. The main personal data processed is the technical data your browser sends to the hosting provider GitHub when it loads a page.

2. Hosting and server logs

Both websites are hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. St., San Francisco, CA 94107, USA.

To deliver a page, your browser has to send technical data to GitHub's servers. This includes your IP address, the date and time of the request, the page requested, the referring page and information about your browser and operating system. According to GitHub, the IP address of every visitor to a GitHub Pages site is logged and stored for security purposes. I have no access to these logs and do not receive any visitor statistics.

  • Purpose: delivering the websites and keeping the service secure and stable.
  • Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR. My legitimate interest is to present these websites reliably and securely.
  • Transfer to the USA: GitHub participates in the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, for which the European Commission has adopted an adequacy decision (Art. 45 GDPR). GitHub also uses the EU standard contractual clauses (Art. 46 GDPR).
  • Encryption: connections to both websites are encrypted with HTTPS (TLS), which protects the transferred data from being read by third parties.
  • Storage period: set by GitHub. Details are in the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

3. No cookies, no tracking, no third-party fonts

Neither website uses cookies, analytics, social media plugins or advertising. The fonts (Space Grotesk and IBM Plex on the skill profile, Inter and JetBrains Mono in the documentation) and scripts such as the three.js library for the 3D illustration are stored on the websites and loaded from there, not from Google or another content delivery network. The skill profile does not use your browser's local storage either; for the documentation, see section 4.

4. The CrashDefNet documentation

  • Settings in your browser: the documentation keeps two small entries in your browser. In local storage, the colour scheme (light or dark): on your first visit it is set to match your system setting, and it changes when you switch it. In session storage, until you close the browser tab, a copy of the repository details shown in the header, so they are not fetched again on every page. Neither entry contains an identifier; the data stays on your device and is not sent to me or anyone else. Storing it is based on § 25 (2) no. 2 TDDDG, as it is needed to display the documentation you requested consistently. You can delete the entries at any time in your browser settings.
  • Search: the search runs entirely in your browser. Search terms are not transmitted.
  • Repository details from GitHub: the header shows details of the CrashDefNet repository on GitHub: the latest version and the number of stars and forks. To show them, your browser fetches these public details directly from the GitHub API (api.github.com) once per browser session, which transmits your IP address to GitHub. The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR; my legitimate interest is to show up-to-date information about the project. What section 2 says about transfers to the USA applies.

5. Links to other websites

Both websites link to other websites, for example LinkedIn, GitHub, arXiv, Zenodo and Google Colab. No data is sent to these providers until you click a link. Once you follow a link, the privacy policy of that website applies.

6. Contact by email

If you write to me by email, I process your email address and the content of your message to answer you. Your message is stored by the provider that hosts my mailbox (recipient category: email provider). The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR (my legitimate interest in answering enquiries) or, if your enquiry concerns a contract or job offer, Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR. I delete the messages once they are no longer needed, unless statutory retention periods apply.

8. Your right to object (Art. 21 GDPR)

I will then no longer process the data concerned, unless I can demonstrate compelling legitimate grounds for the processing which override your interests, rights and freedoms, or the processing serves the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims. An email to the address above is enough to object.
